DevSecOps means integrating security practices into every phase of the software development lifecycle rather than treating security as an afterthought.
Core Principles
• Shift Left: Introduce security checks and threat modeling during the earliest planning and coding stages.
• Shared Responsibility: Make security an active priority for developers, security professionals, and operations teams alike.
• Continuous Automation: Embed automated security gates and scans directly into CI/CD pipelines without slowing down release cycles.

The Malazan Book of the Fallen (/məˈlæzən/) is a series of epic fantasy novels written by the Canadian author Steven Erikson. The series, published by Bantam Books in the U.K. and Tor Books in the U.S., consists of ten volumes, beginning with Gardens of the Moon (1999) and concluding with The Crippled God (2011). Erikson's series presents the narratives of a large cast of characters spanning thousands of years across multiple continents.
